# The website offer, as a Friday post may state it

Mirrors the live page at https://intelliai.au/websites/ (airep `core/views/public.py`:
`WEBSITE_BUILD_PRICE`, `_WEBSITE_PLANS`, `_WEBSITE_ADDONS`, `_WEBSITE_FAQ`). The page is
the published commitment; when this file and the page disagree, fix this file. Every
figure is AUD and ex GST, and a post that quotes one says "+ GST".

## The build

- **$2,500 + GST** ($2,750 including GST), one flat price, fixed, not an estimate: half
  when you say go, half once it is live and you are happy with it.
- What is included: up to ten pages designed around your business rather than a template;
  built for phones first; an enquiry form that lands in your inbox; set up for Google
  (page titles, a sitemap, the local-business details search engines read); your web
  address and email kept working, with links to your old pages sent to the new ones; a
  private link to check it on your phone, and corrections until you are happy; yours to
  keep.
- **Live in about two weeks** once we have your content. Then the second invoice, and the
  monthly starts.
- Replacing a site: we keep your web address and point the old page addresses at the new
  ones, so Google and old links still find you. If your email runs through the same
  company as your current site, we sort that out first.
- Anything bigger (a members' area, a custom system) is quoted in writing before we
  start, never after.

## Then a small monthly

| Plan | A month | What it is | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Simple site | $30 + GST | Pages and an enquiry form, no database to run | Most trade and service businesses |
| Site with a database | $60 + GST | Online bookings, stored enquiries, or a login to edit your own prices, hours and photos | Booking-led businesses, like a driving school |

Priced on what the site runs on (servers, a database), never on visitors or members.
Every plan includes hosting, outage watching, the security padlock renewed, every version
backed up, security updates, and anything that breaks fixed at no charge. The web address
is about $30 a year for a .com.au, registered in the business's name, not ours.

## What you own, always

- You own the site: the words, photos, design files and web address belong to your
  business. Payment and email accounts are opened in your business's name.
- **No lock-in.** The monthly is not a contract: either side can stop it with 30 days'
  notice, and we hand everything over.
- Changes on a simple site: send them to us; most take about fifteen minutes at $70 + GST
  an hour, cost told before we start. Database plans include a login to edit day-to-day
  content yourself.
- A website is a separate job and does not need AIREP. If you run AIREP, the site can show
  prices and products straight from it.
