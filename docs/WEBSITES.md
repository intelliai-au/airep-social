## The build: $2,500 + GST, every feature included

- **$2,500 + GST** ($2,750 including GST) is one flat price. It is fixed, not an estimate.
  Half is paid when you approve it, and half once the site is live and you are happy.
- **Every feature is inside that price** (Keiran, 16 Nov 2026). Picking features changes
  what the site does, never the build price.
- Included in every build:
  - up to ten pages, designed around your business rather than a template
  - built for phones first
  - an enquiry form that lands in your inbox
  - set up for Google: page titles, a sitemap, and the local-business details search
    engines read
  - your web address and email kept working, with links to your old pages sent to the
    new ones
  - a private link to check it on your phone, with corrections until you are happy
  - yours to keep
- **A free live demo in 48 hours**, before you pay anything. If you already have a site,
  your web address is all we need. Without one, we need your business's name, what it
  does and a logo.
- **Live within two weeks** of getting your content. Then the second invoice goes out,
  and the monthly starts.
- **Replacing a site.** We keep your web address and point the old page addresses at the
  new ones, so Google and old links still find you. If your email runs through the same
  company as your current site, we sort that out first.

## The monthly: Hosting and fixes, $120 + GST

There is one plan for every site, named **"Hosting and fixes"** since 17 Nov 2026 (it was
"Looked after"). It covers:

- hosting and its running costs
- the security padlock renewed
- every version backed up
- security updates
- watching for outages
- anything that breaks, fixed at no extra cost

The only thing that changes the monthly is **Email that arrives, +$15 + GST a month**.
Online bookings uses it, so a site with bookings has it too.

Never quote these:

- **$80.** It went to $120 for every site on 16 Nov 2026.
- **"From $120" for a gym or studio.** That separate figure was retired the same day.
- **$30, $60 or any hosting-only price.** Hosting only was withdrawn on 15 Nov 2026.
- **The old add-on setup prices: $150, $200, $350, $500 or $750.** They are gone; every
  feature is inside the build.

Keiran holds the hosting accounts and invoices for them. Never write "run it yourself"
or "the hosting is in your name".

**Price promise.** The monthly can rise only once a year, and any rise is capped at
CPI + 3%, in writing, for as long as you are a customer. The promise covers the monthly
only, not the build or the hourly rate.

**Web address.** A .com.au costs about $30 a year and is registered in the business's
name, not ours. A post that mentions it says "about"; it is never on our invoice.

## Changes, ownership, leaving

- **Changes** after launch are $100 + GST an hour, quoted before we start. Most simple
  changes, like a photo or a number, take about fifteen minutes. Fixing something that
  breaks is part of the monthly, not a change.
- **You own the site:** the words, photos, design files and web address belong to your
  business.
- **No lock-in.** Either side can stop the monthly with 30 days' notice, and we hand
  everything over.
- A website is a separate job and does not need AIREP. If you run AIREP, the site can
  show prices and products straight from it.
